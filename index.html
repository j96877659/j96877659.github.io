Family calendar

Family calendar is a private, home-made wall display built on a Raspberry Pi for one family's own use. It shows the family's Google Calendar events for the week, their chores from Google Tasks, and the local weather.

It is not a public service and is not available for anyone else to sign up to.

Privacy policy

What it accesses. With the account owner's permission, the app reads Google Calendar events and reads and updates tasks in one Google Tasks list (to tick chores off and add the next one for repeating chores).

How the data is used. The data is only used to show the calendar and chores on the display in the family's home, and to mark chores as done when they are tapped on the screen.

Where it is stored. The sign-in token is stored only on the Raspberry Pi in the home. Calendar and task data is fetched from Google and held briefly in the device's memory to draw the screen. Nothing is sent to, sold to, or shared with anyone else, and no data is used for advertising or analytics.

Weather. Weather is fetched from Open-Meteo using only the home's approximate location. No Google data is sent to Open-Meteo.

Removing access. Access can be removed at any time from your Google Account under Security, "Your connections to third-party apps and services", or by disconnecting the account on the display itself.
