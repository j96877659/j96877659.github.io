Privacy policy

Observatory Mirror is a personal project used only by its owner, Jack Webb.

What it accesses

With the owner's permission, it reads (read-only) Google Calendar events and Google Tasks lists from the owner's own Google account, so they can be shown on a mirror at home.

How the data is used

The data is only displayed on the mirror. It is not stored long-term, sold, shared with anyone, or used for any other purpose. The login key is kept only on the owner's own devices.

Removing access

Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
